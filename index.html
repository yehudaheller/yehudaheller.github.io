01Computer Vision · Animal Welfare

Pecking Behaviour Detection & Flock Counting

A YOLO-based keypoint model tracks every bird and the position of its head in real time. When one bird’s head enters another’s body region, the event is classified as a pecking attack and the aggressor is flagged in red — giving researchers an objective, automated measure of flock behaviour.

Outcome
Real-time detection, ID tracking and behaviour classification
Stack
Python · PyTorch · YOLO keypoints

02Hyperspectral Imaging · Sensor Design

Avocado Detection from Just Two Wavelengths

Hyperspectral images capture 204 spectral bands — far more data than a practical field sensor can handle. Using XGBoost decision-tree analysis, I uncovered a direct mathematical relationship between just two wavelengths that is sufficient to detect avocados. That discovery reduces the problem from 204 bands to 2, and now forms the basis of a dedicated two-wavelength camera being developed for avocado detection.

Outcome
204 → 2 wavelengths · 67× faster inference at 89.1% F1-macro
Impact
Foundation for a purpose-built avocado-detection camera
Stack
Hyperspectral imaging · Spectral Python (SPy) · NumPy · XGBoost
